The artifact

A license your agent can actually hold.

Most "AI governance" is a dashboard number. Warrant issues a document — an official, printable certificate for every license an agent earns, bound to the tamper-evident trust-ledger hash. Revocable on a failure, voided on drift, and it decays without fresh evidence. Below is a representative certificate; the live product serves the real one at /certificate/<action>.
