What you're watching

Warrant issues one license per action class. A license needs three things: a Wilson-bounded hit-rate over enough graded outcomes, good calibration (confidence that matches reality), and a brain whose fingerprint hasn't changed. Licenses are revoked on a violated prediction and invalidated on model drift.

Source & live version: github.com/Uthmannabeel/warrant
Evals tell you how smart an agent is. Warrant tells production how much rope to give it — and takes it back.
